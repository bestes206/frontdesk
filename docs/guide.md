# Front Desk: getting set up

Published as the artifact "Front Desk Setup" from the maintainer's account; this file is its source of truth.

About an hour. Everything stays on your own accounts.

## 1. Get Claude
Download the Claude app on your phone and computer (claude.com/download). Sign in or create an account. You need a paid plan (Pro is enough to start) because the front desk runs on a schedule.

## 2. Create a Gmail for the front desk, then connect it
Create a new Gmail account just for this (gmail.com → Create account). It's where copies of your email will go; your real accounts stay exactly as they are. Add it to the Gmail app on your phone. Then in Claude: Settings → Connectors → connect Gmail and Google Calendar using that new account. It's the only account Claude ever reads.

## 3. Turn on notifications
On your phone, allow notifications from the Claude app. That's how it tells you when something needs you.

## 4. Run the setup
Open a new chat in Claude and paste the prompt below. It will ask you about your other email accounts, who always needs a same-day answer, what you never want to see, and which calendar to add appointments to. It shows you real emails from your inbox along the way so you can say "yes, those" or "no, not those."

> Read https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bestes206/frontdesk/main/skills/onboard/SKILL.md and follow it to set up my front desk.

## 5. Forward your other accounts
The setup will tell you which accounts still need forwarding and how. Each one takes a few minutes. Your other email apps keep working exactly as they do now.

## 6. Create the hourly check
The setup ends by handing you a block of text and telling you where to paste it. That creates the hourly check and the page. Run it once by hand and open the page.

## Using it
Open the page before you open your mail. Tap **Open** to read a message, **Handled** when you've dealt with it, **Add** to put a commitment on your calendar, **Always flag** or **Never show** to teach it. It checks every hour and only pings you when something needs you. Tap **Check now** on the page, or tell Claude "run the front desk," when you don't want to wait.

Questions or something it got wrong: contact whoever set this up for you.
